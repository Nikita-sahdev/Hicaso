## /products/appkavo

# Mobile app security audits for developers

AppKavo audits an APK, AAB or IPA and returns a 0–100 security score with ranked findings. It also predicts App Store and Google Play rejections.

Start a project. Try AppKavo.

## What AppKavo does

### AppKavo audits builds without source

Upload an APK, AAB or IPA and get a 0–100 security score with ranked findings.

### Predicts store rejections

Blockers and risks for Google Play and the App Store, before you submit.

### Drafts your privacy paperwork

Data Safety and App Privacy mappings, a privacy policy and terms, from your build.

### AppKavo watches for new vulnerabilities

Every night your dependencies are re-checked against new CVEs, without a new upload.

## How AppKavo works

Three steps from a build to a store-ready app.

### Add your app

Upload an APK, AAB or IPA, add the source code, or give a live URL.

### Read the ranked findings

You get a 0–100 security score, with a fix you can paste for each finding.

### Check the stores and the paperwork

See the blockers and risks Google Play and the App Store may raise, then take the drafted privacy documents.

## AppKavo questions

### Can AppKavo predict App Store and Google Play rejections?

Yes. It lists blockers and risks for Google Play and the App Store before you submit.

### Which privacy documents does AppKavo draft?

Data Safety and App Privacy mappings, a privacy policy and terms, all from your build.

### Do I need to share source code with AppKavo?

No. Upload an APK, AAB or IPA and the audit runs on the compiled build.

### How does AppKavo find new vulnerabilities?

Every night it re-checks your dependencies against new CVEs, without a new upload.

### Who builds AppKavo?

Hicaso builds and runs it, the same team that builds software for clients.

## AppKavo findings with fixes

Every ranked finding comes with a fix you can paste into the file.

# Invoicing software for freelancers and small businesses

BillMinute sends an invoice in 1 minute, in your client's currency, date format and wording. No accounting software, and clients pay you directly.

Start a project. Try BillMinute.

## What BillMinute does

### BillMinute sends an invoice in a minute

Line items, taxes and discounts, from a template or your last invoice.

### BillMinute shows any currency and format

Your client sees their currency, date format and invoice wording.

### BillMinute lets clients pay you directly

By bank transfer or your own pay links. BillMinute never holds payments.

### BillMinute exports your data any time

Invoices, customers and payments to CSV, whenever you want them.

## How BillMinute works

Three steps from a draft to a paid invoice.

### Write the invoice

Add line items, taxes and discounts, from a template or your last invoice.

### Send it in your client's terms

Your client sees the invoice in their currency, date format and wording.

### Get paid directly

Clients pay by bank transfer or your own pay links, and you see what is outstanding, overdue and paid.

## BillMinute questions

### What does my client see on a BillMinute invoice?

The invoice in their currency, date format and wording.

### Does BillMinute hold my payments?

No. Clients pay you directly, by bank transfer or your own pay links.

### Can I export my BillMinute data?

Yes. Export invoices, customers and payments to CSV at any time.

### Who is BillMinute for?

Freelancers and small businesses with clients anywhere.

### Who builds BillMinute?

Hicaso builds and runs it, the same team that builds software for clients.
