## Users

Companies and people who need software built, anywhere in the world (Europe and the UK, the Middle East, the US and Canada, India; confirmed by the owner, 2026-11-18). The site is in English.

- **Primary: startup founders** who need a first version of an app or SaaS product, often without a technical co-founder.
- **Small and mid-size businesses** that need a mobile app, web app, online store or automation for their own operations.
- **Tech teams in larger companies** that need extra developers (a dedicated team), AI features, or an older app modernised.
- **Agencies** that resell development under their own name (white-label).
- **Freelancers and others**, including the people Hicaso's own products serve.

Their job on hicaso.com: decide whether Hicaso can build what they need, then start a project (the enquiry form) or book a free 30-minute call.

## Product Purpose

Hicaso is a custom software development company and product studio in Chandigarh, India. It builds generative and agentic AI, mobile and web software for clients, and runs software products of its own. The website exists to win client projects: success is a qualified enquiry or a booked call.

## Positioning

**A product team builds your product.** The people who ship and run Hicaso's own products, AppKavo and BillMinute, work on client projects (confirmed by the owner as the claim competitors cannot honestly copy). Two working practices go with it: clients see working software in a demo every week, and once a project is paid for, the code, designs and IP belong to the client.

## Product Principles

1. **Show the real thing.** The products Hicaso runs are the proof of its work; show them working rather than describing them.
2. **Every claim checkable.** A fact goes on the site only when the owner has confirmed it; an absence stays absent until real evidence arrives.
3. **Ownership and openness.** The client owns the code and sees progress every week; say so plainly.
4. **One team, start to finish.** From AI and design to testing, cloud and ongoing support, the same team carries the work.
5. **Easy to reach.** A project form, a free call, email, phone and WhatsApp, with no barrier between interest and conversation.
